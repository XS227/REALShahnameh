The Memory

The World Was Fading

جهان داشت از یاد می‌رفت

Three empires fell. The language bent toward silence.

When the courts of Persia were broken and her tongue was bending toward forgetting, one man in the city of Tus sat down and refused to let it go. For thirty years — through poverty, through the silence of kings who never paid him — Ferdowsi wrote. Sixty thousand verses. Every king. Every battle. Every fire.

"I shall not die, for I have sown the seed of words."

— Ferdowsi
